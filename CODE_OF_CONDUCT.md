# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Reqover contributors are expected to keep discussions technical, respectful, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Use clear and constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent unless there is clear evidence otherwise.
- Explain tradeoffs with evidence.
- Respect different experience levels.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Public disclosure of private information.
- Discriminatory language.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block participation when behavior harms the project or contributors.

For urgent conduct issues, contact the repository owner through GitHub.
